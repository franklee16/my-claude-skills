Report for Referee 2

General Comments

The paper studies retail attention and returns. I have three major concerns.

1. My first concern is that the attention measure may proxy for liquidity. Specifically, Wang (2021) shows that
   bid-ask driven liquidity provision generates return predictability, and your measure is correlated with both.
   Please show that your measure has stand-alone content.

1a. A subsidiary point: the sample ends in 2022; please extend through 2024 if possible.

2. Table 3 shows a positive relation between attention and returns. This contradicts Zhang (2022), who finds
   a negative relation on the same universe. Reconcile this or remove the section.

Minor Comments

3. Typo on page 12: "their" should be "there".
