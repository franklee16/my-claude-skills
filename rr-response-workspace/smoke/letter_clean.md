# Responses to the Report from the Referee

We thank the referee sincerely for the comments.

> *1. My first concern is that the attention measure may proxy for liquidity. Specifically, Wang (2021) shows that bid-ask driven liquidity provision generates return predictability, and your measure is correlated with both. Please show that your measure has stand-alone content.*

Thank you for this comment. To evaluate this possibility, we obtain daily liquidity data. We report the results in Table R1 to this response; the coefficient is 0.05 (t-stat. = 3.20). We discuss this issue on page 14 of the revised manuscript, quoted as below: the revised discussion appears in Section 4.2.

> *2. Table 3 shows a positive relation between attention and returns. This contradicts Zhang (2022), who finds a negative relation on the same universe. Reconcile this or remove the section.*

Thank you for raising these important issues. There are two key differences between our empirical specifications and theirs. We replicate their panel in Table R2 to this response and report the full grid; the coefficient is -0.19 (t-stat. = -2.18) in Column (1). Overall, the above comparison suggests that the difference is mainly caused by the measurement choice. We would be pleased to remove Table R2 if you prefer; see Section 5 of the revised manuscript.
